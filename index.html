Taste of Paradise

Online shop for nuts, dried fruit, snacks and bubble tea in Gothenburg, Sweden

I contributed to this project as part of the design team at Zhakvin, a web design and development agency.

Platform
Responsive web
My role
UI/UX design, Zhakvin team
Status
Live

The brief

A local Swedish shop with a wide, varied range needed an online store that feels fresh and natural and makes it quick to find and buy products.

Approach & key decisions

  • Grouped a large assortment into four clear entry points (speciality products, snacks, drinks, healthy snacks) so shoppers can start browsing from the first screen.
  • Made prices, discounts and add-to-cart visible on every product card to shorten the path to purchase.
  • Used a time-limited offer, new arrivals and gift packages to give returning visitors a reason to explore.
  • Designed the whole interface in Swedish and added familiar payment badges to build trust with local customers.

Petro Energy (GPE)

Corporate website for a manufacturer of storage tanks and loading equipment for oil, gas and petrochemicals

Platform
Responsive web
My role
UI/UX design
Audience
B2B / industrial

The brief

An engineering company with highly technical products wanted a modern website that builds credibility with industrial clients and explains a complex product range clearly.

Approach & key decisions

  • Opened with clear proof points (clients, years of experience, completed projects) so visitors see credibility within seconds.
  • Structured dense technical information into product cards, client logos, an FAQ and a company history timeline, so each type of visitor finds their answer quickly.
  • Added an export map and news section to show international reach and ongoing activity.
  • Chose a calm, corporate blue palette and large industrial photography to match the expectations of B2B buyers.

LuxeShop

E-commerce site for a Danish skincare and haircare brand inspired by Persian herbal remedies

Platform
Responsive web
My role
UI/UX design
Market
Denmark

The brief

A young brand with natural, premium products needed a shop that tells its story and earns trust from customers who are new to it.

Approach & key decisions

  • Built the visual language around soft herbal greens and editorial photography to express "natural" and "premium" at the same time.
  • Placed the brand story high on the page, followed by favourite products, so first-time visitors understand who the brand is before they shop.
  • Answered common doubts directly with a "Why choose us" section (natural ingredients, vegan and cruelty-free) and customer reviews.
  • Added a beauty journal and newsletter to support content marketing and repeat visits.

Scroll inside the screen to see the full page

Duraniq Flooring Group

Website for a flooring supply and installation company in Melbourne, Australia

Platform
Responsive web
My role
UI/UX design
Market
Australia

The brief

A flooring company with over 15 years of experience wanted a premium website that showcases its products and finished projects and turns visitors into quote requests.

Approach & key decisions

  • Used warm wood tones, a dark palette and large interior photography so the website feels as premium as the floors themselves.
  • Put a clear "Get a free quote" call to action in the hero and kept services simple: supply and installation, sanding and polishing.
  • Organised the product range by brand (Serflor, Polyflor, Armstrong) with tabs, so visitors can compare collections without leaving the page.
  • Built trust with a project gallery, client testimonials, partner brand logos and an FAQ that answers common questions before people call.

PHG

Responsive website, desktop and mobile, for a healthcare company in the UAE that imports and exports medicines and medical equipment

Platform
Desktop & mobile
My role
UI/UX design
Sector
Healthcare

The brief

A healthcare trading company needed to present a broad set of services and its quality standards clearly to business partners, on desktop as well as on their phones.

Approach & key decisions

  • Designed both desktop and mobile layouts, keeping one clear message per section and large touch targets on mobile.
  • Organised the offer into strategic business units so partners can jump straight to the service they need.
  • Gave certifications, supply-chain management and production quality their own sections, because trust and compliance matter most in healthcare.
  • Ended with direct contact options and a location map to make the next step easy.
